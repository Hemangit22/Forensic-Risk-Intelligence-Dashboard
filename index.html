Technical Workflow

End-to-End Data Pipeline

Multi-source ingestion of 1M+ audit logs. Complex joins, null remediation, temporal feature engineering across Kaggle & Feedzai datasets.

SQL
Window functions & CTEs to calculate Time-to-Risk. Risk segmentation by velocity and impact. Big 4 firm behavioural pattern extraction.

Strategic Findings

What the Data Revealed

⚡
The 1.0-Day Gap
Analysis revealed an average 1.0-day Time-to-Risk — proving manual audits are structurally too slow to intercept modern fraud at scale.
🎯
The 10-Day Threshold
68% of incidents occur within the first 10 days. Velocity-Gating at this threshold is the optimal point for automated intervention.
🔄
The Efficiency Paradox
High employee workloads (notably Deloitte) directly correlate with higher risk exposure — the firms working hardest are most vulnerable.
